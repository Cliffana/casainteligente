Frequently Asked Questions

Are smart kitchen appliances worth it?
Yes, especially if you want convenience and energy savings. A smart kettle costs 35€ and lets you boil water at the right temperature for each drink. A smart air fryer saves up to 67% energy compared to a traditional oven. The initial investment is small and the convenience payoff is immediate.
Do I need a hub to use these appliances?
Most work with just Wi-Fi and the manufacturer's app (Mi Home, VeSync, etc.). If you have a voice assistant like Alexa or Google Home, you can control everything by voice without any additional hub.
Which smart kitchen appliance should I buy first?
The Xiaomi Smart Kettle Pro at 35€. It's the cheapest, most useful daily appliance, and you'll immediately see how convenient app control and timers are. After that, a smart air fryer is the natural next step.
Do smart kitchen appliances use a lot of energy?
No, many have eco modes and sensors that optimize consumption. A smart air fryer, for example, cooks 46% faster than a traditional one, using much less energy. You can also schedule it to run during cheaper electricity hours.
Do they work with Alexa and Google Home?
Yes, all the models we recommend are compatible with Alexa and Google Assistant. You can ask Alexa to heat the kettle or turn on the air fryer.
