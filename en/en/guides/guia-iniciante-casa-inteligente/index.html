What is a smart home?

A smart home is a residence equipped with internet-connected devices that can be controlled remotely or automated. From turning on the lights with a voice command to automatically adjusting the thermostat when you leave the house, smart home technology makes your everyday life more convenient, efficient, and secure.

The smart home market in Portugal grew by more than 35% in 2025, with more and more people discovering the benefits of home automation. And the good news? Getting started has never been more affordable.

Where to begin? Budget and priorities

Before buying your first device, answer three questions:

  1. What is your initial budget? — We recommend starting with €50 to €100
  2. Which virtual assistant do you prefer? — Alexa (Amazon), Google Assistant, or Apple HomeKit
  3. What problem do you want to solve first? — Lighting, security, energy savings?

Tip: Start small. An Amazon Echo Dot and a smart outlet are enough to feel the real impact of automation in your daily life.

Smart speaker — the center of your ecosystem

The smart speaker is the most important device. It serves as a voice assistant, control hub, and music speaker all in one. The Amazon Echo Dot 5th generation is our top recommendation for beginners thanks to its excellent value for money.

Smart outlets — the gateway to automation

With a smart outlet, you can turn any "dumb" device into a smart one. Switch it on and off remotely, set schedules, and monitor energy consumption. The TP-Link Tapo P115 is the best entry-level option.

Protocols: WiFi, Zigbee, Z-Wave, and Matter

Understanding protocols is essential for making the right choices:

  • WiFi — Devices connect directly to your network. Easy to set up, but uses more bandwidth.
  • Zigbee — Low-power mesh network. Requires a hub, but is more reliable when managing many devices.
  • Z-Wave — Similar to Zigbee, but less common in Europe.
  • Matter — The new universal standard. Matter devices work with any assistant.

Step by step: setting up your first device

  1. Buy your Echo Dot on Amazon.es at the best price
  2. Plug it in and wait for the orange LED
  3. Open the Alexa app on your smartphone and follow the instructions
  4. Connect to your WiFi network when prompted by the app
  5. Run your first test: "Alexa, what time is it?"
  6. Add a smart outlet and say "Alexa, discover devices"
  7. Create a routine: "Alexa, good night" → turn off lights and lock doors

Simple automations to get started

  • Good night: One command turns off all lights and outlets
  • Good morning: Gradually brighten the lights and get the weather forecast
  • Leaving home: Turn off devices and activate away mode
  • Arriving home: Turn on the entry lights and deactivate the alarm

Common beginner mistakes

  • Buying too many devices at once without testing the ecosystem first
  • Ignoring compatibility between brands
  • Not updating device firmware
  • Putting all devices on the same network without segmentation
  • Overlooking security: always use strong passwords and 2FA whenever possible
