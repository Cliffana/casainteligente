TL;DR — Key Takeaways

  • Xiaomi/Aqara offers better build quality and premium design
  • Tuya/Smart Life has more budget-friendly options and greater variety
  • Both work with Alexa and Google Home — compatibility is not an issue
  • For Home Assistant, Xiaomi via Zigbee has more stable integration
  • You can mix devices from both ecosystems without any problems

Introduction

Xiaomi (through its Aqara sub-brand) and Tuya (through the Smart Life app) are two of the largest smart home ecosystems in the world. Both offer dozens of devices at accessible prices, but with different approaches. In this comparison, we break down everything you need to know to choose the best one for you.

Xiaomi/Aqara: Pros and Cons

Xiaomi entered the smart home market through Aqara, focusing on build quality and design.

Pros: - Premium materials (glass, aluminum, high-quality plastic) - Minimalist, elegant design - Very stable Zigbee gateway - Accurate and responsive sensors - Robust Home Assistant integration

Cons: - Slightly higher price point - Smaller product variety - Mi Home app can be confusing - Reliance on Chinese cloud servers for some devices

Tuya/Smart Life: Pros and Cons

Tuya is an open platform used by dozens of brands, resulting in a wide range of products.

Pros: - Very competitive prices - Large variety of devices - Intuitive Smart Life app - Works with Alexa, Google, and Siri - Supports Matter on newer devices

Cons: - Variable build quality - Design isn't always premium - Cloud dependency for many functions - Inconsistent customer support

Compatibility with Alexa, Google, and HomeKit

With Alexa: Both work great. Xiaomi requires the "Xiaomi Home" or "Aqara Home" skill. Tuya uses the "Smart Life" skill.

With Google Home: Works well with both. Xiaomi requires linking your Mi Home account. Tuya uses your Smart Life account.

With HomeKit: Xiaomi Aqara has hubs compatible with HomeKit. Tuya offers some devices with native HomeKit support.

Home Assistant: Which One Works Better?

For Home Assistant users:

  • Xiaomi Aqara: Integration via Zigbee gateway is excellent. The Xiaomi Gateway 3 add-on enables full local control without cloud dependency.
  • Tuya: The official Tuya integration for Home Assistant works but has limited API quotas. The local alternative (Tuya Local) works for some Wi-Fi devices.

Verdict: Xiaomi Aqara wins for Home Assistant thanks to its more stable local integration.

Which One Should You Choose in 2026?

Choose Xiaomi Aqara if: - You value build quality - You use Home Assistant - You prefer premium design - You want more accurate sensors

Choose Tuya if: - You're on a tight budget - You want a wider variety of products - You prefer the Smart Life app - You want to experiment without a big investment

Both ecosystems are excellent. The good news is that you don't have to pick just one — you can mix devices and use whichever assistant you prefer to control everything.
