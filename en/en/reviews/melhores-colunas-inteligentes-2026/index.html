Introduction

Choosing the right smart speaker can be tricky. Amazon, Google, and Apple compete in this market with different approaches, each with its own strengths. In this review, we compare the main models to help you decide which one to buy in 2026.

Amazon Echo Dot 5th Generation

The Amazon Echo Dot 5th Generation remains the best-selling smart speaker in Portugal, and for good reason.

Pros: - Unbeatable price (€35–50 on sale) - Excellent sound quality for its size - Built-in temperature sensor - Alexa available in multiple languages - Thousands of Skills available - Compatible with most smart home devices

Cons: - Sound doesn't fill large rooms - Simple design (fabric only) - Privacy: microphones are always listening

Google Nest Audio

The Google Nest Audio is the direct alternative to the Echo Dot, with a focus on sound quality.

Pros: - Best sound in its class (50W vs. 30W on the Echo Dot) - Premium design in recycled fabric - Integration with Google services (Calendar, Maps, Gmail) - Google Assistant in multiple languages

Cons: - More expensive (€70–90) - Fewer compatible smart home devices - Screen only available on the Nest Hub

Apple HomePod Mini

For those already invested in the Apple ecosystem, the HomePod Mini is the natural choice.

Pros: - Surprisingly good sound for its size - Seamless integration with iPhone, iPad, and Mac - Siri works great with HomeKit - Compact and elegant design

Cons: - Higher price point (€100–110) - Limited to Apple users - Less smart home compatibility

Which One to Choose Based on Your Ecosystem

  • Amazon Prime user — Echo Dot or Echo Studio (best integration with Prime Music, Video, and shopping)
  • Google user — Nest Audio (Calendar, Gmail, Maps built in)
  • Apple user — HomePod Mini (AirPlay, Handoff, HomeKit)
  • Just want great sound — Echo Studio (best overall sound)
  • Tight budget — Echo Dot 5th Gen (best value for money)

Final Verdict

The Amazon Echo Dot 5th Generation is our pick for most people. It offers the best balance of price, features, and sound quality.

If sound is your top priority, the Google Nest Audio or the Echo Studio are better choices. For Apple users, the HomePod Mini is worth the extra investment.

Recommendation: Start with an Echo Dot and expand as needed.
